# Research Report: as agnetic AI booming what is future of programmer ?

The future of programming will involve collaboration between humans and AI, with programmers playing a key role in designing, overseeing, and refining AI systems. The role of programmers will evolve to include working alongside AI tools to enhance productivity and solve complex problems, rather than being replaced by AI.

Programmers will continue to be essential in developing and maintaining AI systems, as AI cannot replace the human elements such as creativity, critical thinking, and problem-solving. According to a study from McKinsey & Co., up to 80% of programming jobs will remain human-centric, highlighting the continued demand for skilled programmers. Continuous learning and adaptability will be crucial for programmers to stay relevant in an AI-driven world.

Programmers should embrace AI as a tool to augment their capabilities rather than replace them. This includes using AI to automate repetitive tasks, improve code quality, and assist with data analysis, while focusing on areas where human judgment and innovation are most valuable. The future of programming will be characterized by collaboration between humans and AI, with programmers playing an integral role in shaping the direction of technology.
